A little about me

Great product leadership creates clarity from complexity, gives teams real ownership, and builds an environment of trust, psychological safety, and continuous improvement where people do their best work.

I'm a product leader who builds data product ecosystems: the APIs, platforms, and delivery channels that get data into the hands of developers, businesses, and AI. Data products became my niche because they take a rare skill. Data has no UI. You can't click around to understand it, so you have to picture the whole system in your head and clearly articulate the problem you're solving. I have an instinct for breaking complex problems down to their simplest form, which makes them easier to explain, teach, and rally a team around.

That instinct comes from a degree in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from Stony Brook University. Math taught me to find the pattern underneath the noise and reduce it to something clear. To me, product management follows the scientific method: observe, form a hypothesis, experiment, and measure.

Today I lead product strategy, execution, and go-to-market operating models, helping organizations scale how they build and deliver value. Just as important, I've spent more than a decade honing the craft of leadership: studying management best practices, putting them to work, and refining what works. The result is teams where people feel safe to speak up, take ownership, and get a little better every release.

AccuWeather · 2025–2026

Lightning Data Suite™

I turned a lightning detection network, acquired to cut data costs, into a revenue-generating data product. It offers forecast, real-time, and historical lightning data through APIs, datasets, and webhooks.

0 → 1Data productAPIMonetizationGo-to-market
3delivery channels: APIs, datasets & webhooks

Problem

In 2024, AccuWeather acquired a lightning detection network to reduce data acquisition costs. Developers and enterprise customers valued lightning data, but there was no product strategy, monetization model, or distribution channel for it.

Solution

Customer and developer discovery validated high-value use cases: automated lightning alerts for outdoor operations, grid impact assessment for utilities, and operational planning based on lightning risk. I led strategy, prioritization, and execution across technology, data science, sales, and marketing to build a developer-friendly platform that delivers real-time, forecast, and historical lightning data directly into the systems customers already work in.

Result

Generated new ACV in early access from a data asset that had never been sold. The launch also rounded out AccuWeather's enterprise data portfolio with a complete lightning suite.

Lessons learned

The launch drew strong interest, but it also showed a brand gap: AccuWeather isn't known for lightning data, so buyers don't think of us first. We prioritized a full brand campaign for 2027 to build that association and turn awareness into demand.

What I shipped

Marketing site & positioning ↗

Defined target personas, buyer use cases, differentiation, competitive positioning, and core messaging. This became the basis for enterprise sales and marketing across web, decks, collateral, and customer conversations.

Developer resources ↗

Led the developer experience strategy: API requirements, documentation standards, provisioning workflows, pricing alignment, and operational readiness for enterprise adoption on AccuWeather's API platform.

Candid · 2024–2025

Charity Check: California AB 488

A state-level nonprofit verification product, modeled on Candid's federal tool Charity Check. It gives donation platforms real-time access to nonprofit status so they can comply with California's AB 488.

ComplianceAPISaaSData pipelineRegulatory
+15%revenue
+100%API usage YoY

Problem

A 2024 California law requires donation platforms to verify that nonprofits are in good standing with the Attorney General and the Franchise Tax Board. The only way to check was a government website, so platforms delayed building it in.

Solution

I saw the opportunity for a state-level version of Charity Check. Working with the VP of Data and engineering, I built an automated data pipeline and a consent capture tool to give platforms real-time API access. We then added it to our SaaS tool to create a multi-channel compliance suite.

Result

A 15% increase in revenue and a 100% increase in API usage year over year.

What I shipped

Charity Check SaaS: state-level verification ↗

Compliance managers check a nonprofit's federal and state status when accepting donations, and keep a record for legal audits. We redesigned the SaaS tool to verify both and save the result to their systems of record, as proof the donation was legitimate when it was made.

Charity Check API: state-level verification ↗

Donation platforms needed real-time access to nonprofits' state registration to stay compliant. I designed a new state-level verification endpoint and a pipeline that automatically pulls updates from the government.
