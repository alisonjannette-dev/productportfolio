A little about me

Good product leadership is mostly clarity, curiosity, and caring a lot about the people on both sides of the screen.

I've spent more than a decade building products across B2B SaaS, consumer, and platform businesses. I've led teams from zero to one and from one to scale. That has meant defining strategy, hiring and coaching product managers, and working closely with engineering, design, data, and go-to-market.

My sweet spot is the messy middle: when a company knows it needs to grow but isn't sure where to bet. I get close to customers, find the few things that matter, and build the operating rhythm that lets teams ship with confidence.

Outside the roadmap, I'm the person who remembers everyone's coffee order and brings a surprisingly strong opinion to team offsites.

✦ After-hours fact: I have a sourdough starter named Kevin, a ranked list of every airport snack I've tried, and a weakness for karaoke power ballads.

Start with the problem

Fall in love with the customer's problem, not our solution. Discovery is a habit, not a phase.

Make the strategy legible

If the team can't explain why we're building something, it isn't a strategy yet.
