A little about me

Great product leadership starts with creating clarity from complexity, empowering teams through ownership, and building an environment where people can do their best work.

I'm a product leader specializing in API, data, and platform products. I came to product management because I love solving complex problems, I think in data, and I'm curious about how businesses, technology, and people connect. Four years into my career I found my way into API and data product management, where my strengths meet: turning business challenges into scalable solutions, using data to guide decisions, and aligning teams around a shared vision.

I have a degree in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from Stony Brook University, and I've always been drawn to patterns, systems, and turning information into insight. To me, product management looks a lot like the scientific method: observe patterns, form a hypothesis, experiment, and measure the outcome.

Over my career I've grown from solving individual product problems to leading product strategy, shaping operating models, and helping organizations scale how they build and deliver value. I like bringing structure to ambiguity, connecting business goals with technology opportunities, and helping teams focus on the problems that matter most.

AccuWeather · 2025–present

Lightning Data Suite™

I turned a lightning detection network, acquired to cut data costs, into a revenue-generating data product. It offers forecast, real-time, and historical lightning data through APIs, datasets, and webhooks.

0 → 1Data productAPIMonetizationGo-to-market
~$400KACV pipeline in early access
3delivery channels: APIs, datasets & webhooks

Problem

In 2024, AccuWeather acquired a lightning detection network to reduce data acquisition costs. Developers and enterprise customers valued lightning data, but there was no product strategy, monetization model, or distribution channel for it.

Solution

Customer and developer discovery validated high-value use cases: automated lightning alerts for outdoor operations, grid impact assessment for utilities, and operational planning based on lightning risk. I led strategy, prioritization, and execution across technology, data science, sales, and marketing.

Result

About $400K in ACV pipeline during early access. That opened a new way to make money from a data asset that had never been sold, and filled out AccuWeather's enterprise data portfolio with a complete lightning suite.

What I shipped

Marketing site & positioning

Defined target personas, buyer use cases, differentiation, competitive positioning, and core messaging. This became the basis for enterprise sales and marketing across web, decks, collateral, and customer conversations.

Developer resources

Led the developer experience strategy: API requirements, documentation standards, provisioning workflows, pricing alignment, and operational readiness for enterprise adoption on AccuWeather's API platform.

Candid · 2024–2025

Charity Check: California AB 488

A state-level nonprofit verification product, modeled on Candid's federal tool Charity Check. It gives donation platforms real-time access to nonprofit status so they can comply with California's AB 488.

ComplianceAPISaaSData pipelineRegulatory
+15%revenue
+100%API usage YoY

Problem

A 2024 California law requires donation platforms to verify that nonprofits are in good standing with the Attorney General and the Franchise Tax Board. The only way to check was a government website, so platforms delayed building it in.

Solution

I saw the opportunity for a state-level version of Charity Check. Working with the VP of Data and engineering, I built an automated data pipeline and a consent capture tool to give platforms real-time API access. We then added it to our SaaS tool to create a multi-channel compliance suite.

Result

A 15% increase in revenue and a 100% increase in API usage year over year.

What I shipped

Charity Check SaaS: state-level verification

Compliance managers check a nonprofit's federal and state status when accepting donations, and keep a record for legal audits. We redesigned the SaaS tool to verify both and save the result to their systems of record, as proof the donation was legitimate when it was made.

Charity Check API: state-level verification

Donation platforms needed real-time access to nonprofits' state registration to stay compliant. I designed a new state-level verification endpoint and a pipeline that automatically pulls updates from the government.
